How sessions work

  • Attendance % = present days ÷ working days in session up to today
  • Sundays are always excluded — no attendance is marked on Sundays
  • Holidays you add are excluded from the denominator
  • Click Browse to see students grouped by branch → section
  • Students are auto-matched to sessions by admission year + course
  • Each course can have exactly one active session at a time
